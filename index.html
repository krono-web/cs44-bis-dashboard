About this datasetScope, scoring and interpretation+

One consistent snapshot

Batch 3, captured on 11 September 2026, reclassified on 7 October 2026 against the same saved text. Semester 2 is preferred, with other 2026 sessions allowed. The 2025 INFS3080 fallback and SIE units are not included in this snapshot.

Automated evidence signals

Eight categories and 48 scoring rules (taxonomy v6.3). The seven previously proposed rules are now active, and the client's 28 September phrase additions are included. Generic wording is carried as review-only signals so that it cannot create a positive result on its own. A positive unit has at least one positive evidence item. Review-only signals stay separate from positive results.

Human review is pending

These are rule-based findings from public text, not teaching-quality ratings. No completed human reviews are recorded. Missing outlines are excluded from percentage denominators; no match is not proof that a teaching method is absent.

Rules: taxonomy v6.3 (2026-10-07-v6.3-client-review), 48 scoring rules. Text: the saved batch-3 capture of 11 September 2026. Scores: produced by src/run_pipeline.py against that saved text, not by a database run — the matching database batch has not been generated yet, so these figures cannot be reconciled against database/reports/ until it is. Every total shown here is a sum of the per-unit, per-category records in this file; the page never re-scores text in the browser. Previous source reports ↗
